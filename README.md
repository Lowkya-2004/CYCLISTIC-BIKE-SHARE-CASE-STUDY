# CYCLISTIC-BIKE-SHARE-CASE-STUDY
Data analysis case study exploring Cyclistic bike-share usage patterns to understand rider behavior and identify opportunities to convert casual riders into annual members.

# Project Overview

The Cyclistic Bike-Share Case Study is a data analysis project focused on understanding how different types of riders use Cyclistic's bike-sharing service. The primary objective is to analyze the differences in riding patterns and behaviors between casual riders and annual members and provide data-driven insights that can help Cyclistic increase the number of annual memberships.


# Business Objective

Cyclistic wants to maximize the number of annual memberships.

The key business question is:

How do annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently?

By identifying these differences, the company can develop targeted marketing strategies to encourage casual riders to become annual members.


# Rider Types

The analysis focuses on two main customer categories:

Casual Riders – Customers who purchase single-ride or day passes.
Annual Members – Customers who purchase annual memberships.


# Six Phases of Data Analysis
Ask – Define the business problem and objectives.
Prepare – Collect and organize the bike-share data.
Process – Clean and transform the data for analysis.
Analyze – Identify patterns and differences between rider types.
Share – Present insights using charts and visualizations.
Act – Provide recommendations to convert casual riders into annual members.
